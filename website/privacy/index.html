The app

OrcBlock has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. Your list of sites, your timer and your potions are kept on your Mac and never sent to us or anyone else. There is no server of ours for it to talk to.

The only time the app goes online is to fetch the small icon of each site on your list, straight from that site, the same way your browser would. It sends nothing else along.

This website

No cookies, no trackers, no analytics. The only thing it remembers is your light or dark choice, and that stays in your own browser. The site is served by Cloudflare, which handles requests like any web host; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Downloads

The download button links to the release on GitHub, which serves the file. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. Found a security problem? Please report it privately, as the security policy explains.
